## 1. Investigation of Regression Delay-Band MAE Discrepancy

### 1.1 Question Under Audit
A discrepancy was observed between:
- **Stage 4.1 empirical report:** Random Forest regression MAE for actual delay $\le 0$ min = **$12.19$ minutes**.
- **Stage 5.1 draft narrative:** Random Forest regression MAE for actual delay $\le 0$ min cited as **$9.79$ minutes**.

### 1.2 Forensic Investigation Findings
1. **Underlying Data File & Plot:**
   - [`results/regression/stage4_error_by_delay_band.csv`](file:///Users/arnavraj/Downloads/flight-delay-propagation/results/regression/stage4_error_by_delay_band.csv) (generated during Stage 4.1 robustness audit) records:
     - Band: `Early / On-Time (<= 0m)`
     - Observations: $N = 734,735$ ($64.40\%$ of full validation set)
     - Model: `Random Forest Regressor (Group C)`
     - MAE: **$12.19$ minutes**
     - RMSE: **$15.57$ minutes**
     - Mean Prediction Bias: **$+11.43$ minutes**
   - The diagnostic figure [`results/figures/regression_error_by_delay_band.png`](file:///Users/arnavraj/Downloads/flight-delay-propagation/results/figures/regression_error_by_delay_band.png) was rendered directly from this CSV and shows the bar for Random Forest at $12.19$ minutes.
2. **Model & Prediction Integrity Check:**
   - Evaluated both [`models/regression/random_forest_group_c.joblib`](file:///Users/arnavraj/Downloads/flight-delay-propagation/models/regression/random_forest_group_c.joblib) (Stage 4) and [`models/final/final_selected_regressor.joblib`](file:///Users/arnavraj/Downloads/flight-delay-propagation/models/final/final_selected_regressor.joblib) (Stage 5) against `data/processed/modeling_validation.parquet` transformed with `models/final/final_preprocessor_group_c.joblib`.
   - Predictions are identical across all $1,140,909$ rows (`np.allclose = True`).
   - On the subset of flights with actual delay $\le 0$ min ($N = 734,735$):
     - Empirical MAE: **$12.1856$ minutes** ($\approx 12.19$ min)
     - Empirical RMSE: **$15.5651$ minutes** ($\approx 15.57$ min)
     - Empirical Mean Bias: **$+11.4293$ minutes** ($\approx +11.43$ min)
   - On the same subset for `models/regression/mlp_regressor_group_c.joblib` ($\alpha = 0.0$):
     - Empirical MAE: **$9.3598$ minutes** ($\approx 9.36$ min)
     - Empirical RMSE: **$12.41$ minutes**
     - Empirical Mean Bias: **$+4.29$ minutes**
3. **Root Cause:**
   - The figure `$9.79$ min` was purely a typographical error in the narrative prose of Section 6.5 in the Stage 5.1 draft report. It was never generated by any model evaluation script, nor did it exist in any CSV artifact.
4. **Resolution:**
   - Corrected Section 6.5 in [`results/tables/stage5_integrity_audit.md`](file:///Users/arnavraj/Downloads/flight-delay-propagation/results/tables/stage5_integrity_audit.md) to document the verified empirical MAE of **$12.19$ minutes** for Random Forest (alongside MLP at $9.36$ min, Decision Tree at $12.02$ min, and OLS at $16.04$ min).
   - No data files or figures required regeneration, as `stage4_error_by_delay_band.csv` and `regression_error_by_delay_band.png` already contain the verified $12.19$ min figure.
